# Retail Store Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

The retail business has sales and order data, but the raw data does not clearly show how the business is performing.

The analysis aims to understand **sales, cost, profit, orders, products, delivery, and returns** and identify areas that need improvement.

## 2. Business Objective

The main objective is to use Excel to analyze retail data and provide useful insights for better business decisions.

## 3. Business Questions

The analysis focuses on:

* What is the total sales revenue?
* What is the total cost and profit?
* Which products/categories perform best?
* Which products have lower profitability?
* How many orders are completed and returned?
* What is the return rate?
* What is the average delivery time?
* Which countries/locations generate more sales?
* Which payment methods are commonly used?
* Are there any important business problems or opportunities?

## 4. Key KPIs

* Total Sales Revenue
* Total Cost
* Net Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Completed Orders
* Returned Orders
* Return Rate
* Average Delivery Time
* Average Order Value
